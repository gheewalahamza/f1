ABOUT FORMULA 1

Formula One (F1) is the top level of car racing in the world. Cars race on special tracks and city streets. The word "formula" means a strict set of rules that all cars and engines must follow.

| HOW FORMULA 1 WORKS |

  • Teams:Eleven teams compete, and each team builds two cars.
  • Drivers:Two drivers race for each team, meaning twenty-two drivers race in total.
  • Races:A season has 24 races, called Grands Prix, held in different countries all over the world.
  • Weekend Schedule: Events span a few days, starting with practice[friday], then time trials (qualifying) to pick starting spots[saturday], and finally the main race[sunday].

    | Winning and Points |

  • Drivers Championship:The driver who gets the most points across the year wins a world title.
  • Constructors Championship:The team that accumulates the most points across the year wins the constructors' title.
  • Points System:Points are awarded based on finishing positions in each race, with the winner receiving the most points.

    | Cars and Rules |

  • Speed:The cars are very fast, light, and powerful, reaching speeds over 320 km/h.
  • Design:Every team must design and build its own car inside the official rules.
  • Pit stops: Teams change all four tires and fix minor issues in just about two seconds during a race stop.

    Quick F1 facts

  1. Origin:The first Formula 1 World Championship was held in 1950 in silverstone, england.
  2. Tires:there are 5 types of tires used in F1 races, depending on track conditions. F1 tires use nitrogen gas instead of common air
  3. Drivers:All of the F1 Drivers are billionaires. their physical fitness is built to fight extreme conditions like G-force, critical thinking, claustophopobia, and extreme heat.

Why F1 is Special

Formula 1 is special because it is the ultimate fusion of elite human athleticism, death-defying speed, and cutting-edge engineering, where only a tiny group of elite drivers pilot the world's fastest racing machines across global circuits, turning high-stakes split-second team strategies into a thrilling global spectacle.
