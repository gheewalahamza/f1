Ferrari

Country: Italy

Founded: 1950

Drivers: Charles Leclerc, Lewis Hamilton

The only team to have competed in every season since the world championship began, the Prancing Horse has grown from the humble dream of founder Enzo Ferrari to become one of the most iconic and recognised brands in the world.

Mercedes

Country: United Kingdom

Founded: 1970

Drivers: kimi antonelli, george russell

Mercedes's modern F1 revival started with the creation of a works squad for 2010 - the platform for a meteoric rise up the Grand Prix order. The team generated huge excitement from the off with the sensational return of Michael Schumacher

Mclaren

Country: United Kingdom

Founded: 1966

Drivers: Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri

Since entering the sport in 1966 under the guidance and restless endeavour of eponymous founder Bruce, McLaren's success has been nothing short of breathtaking. Five glittering decades have yielded countless victories, pole positions and podiums.

Red Bull Racing

Country:United Kingdom

Founded: 1997

Drivers: Max Verstappen, Isack Hadjar

Red Bull were no strangers to F1 - as sponsors - prior to formally entering as a works team in 2004. Nonetheless, the scale of their success over the following decade was staggering.

Racing Bull

Country: Italy

Founded: 1985

Drivers:Liam Lawson, Arvid Lindblad

Established in 2006 as a squad in which young drivers from Red Bull’s prodigious talent pool could cut their F1 teeth, Racing Bulls – originally named Toro Rosso, then AlphaTauri, then RB – were formed from the ashes of the plucky Minardi team.

Alpine

Country:United Kingdom

Founded: 1986

Drivers: Pierre Gasly, franco colapatino

Alpine may be a relatively new name to Formula 1, but Renault’s famous sportscar arm has plenty of motorsport heritage. The 2021 rebrand of the team marked the next step in Renault’s F1 revival.

Aston Martin

Country: United Kingdom

Founded: 2018

Drivers: Fernando Alonso, Lance Stroll

Aston Martin’s original Formula 1 foray – over half a century ago – lasted just five races. This time, though, it’s serious. This F1 squad are no strangers to success, having won in their original guise of Jordan and most recently as Racing Point in 2020.
